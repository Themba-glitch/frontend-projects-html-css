About Us

Safe Haven is a non-profit organization and a women's shelter dedicated to serving women and girls who have experienced sexual abuse and domestic violence or any other form of Gender-Based Violence.

Our mission is to offer a safe, compassionate, and empowering space for healing, support, and restoration. Established in 2024, Safe Haven has successfully restored and empowered many women who have fallen victim to the current epidemic that has crowded our country in recent years.

The NGO aims to expand its reach to greater heights. The aim is to grow and branch into every province, helping all women in need.
