Welcome to Hope Haven

At Hope Haven, we believe every woman deserves safety, dignity, and a chance to rebuild her life. Our shelter offers protection, emotional support, skills training, and a community of care for women who have experienced abuse or trauma.

Our Services

We offer a full range of support to help women heal, empower themselves, and rebuild their lives.

  • Safe and Secure Temporary Housing
  • Counseling & Emotional Support
  • Legal Advice & Advocacy
  • Skills Development & Job Training
  • Medical Referrals & Health Care
  • Child Care & Educational Assistance
